## Publicações

- **[Benchmarking Spatial, Spectral, and Self-Supervised Cues for Face Forgery Detection under Realistic Degradation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01511)** · SIBGRAPI 2026 · [código](https://github.com/lucasdocunha/FaceForgery-Benchmark)
  Compara seis famílias de modelos em dados limpos e degradados. O DINOv3 congelado teve o melhor resultado sob degradação (ROC-AUC de 0,726).
- **[An Ensemble-Based Self-Taught Learning Approach for Parking Space Classification Under Limited Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03258)** · IEEE SMC 2026 · [código](https://github.com/lucasdocunha/PKLot-Ensemble-STL)
